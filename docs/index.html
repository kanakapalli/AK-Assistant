Dictation

Faster than typing, and it fixes the ums

Words appear in the popup as you speak. When you let go, the final text is pasted where your cursor is, and your clipboard is put back.

  • Parakeet models on the Neural Engine add punctuation and capitals for you
  • 25 languages, plus Whisper for Hindi, Telugu, Tamil and 90+ more
  • Filler words like “um” and “uh” are removed
  • Your speakers are muted while you talk, so videos and calls don't end up in the text
  • Every dictation is saved in a searchable History on your Mac

AI assistant

A private AI that answers out loud and runs your Mac

Hold the AI key and ask anything. A chat model on your Mac answers in a few seconds and a natural voice reads it out. Follow-up questions keep the context.

open Xcode set a timer for 25 minutes remind me at 5 pm to call mom volume 30 search YouTube for lofi what's my battery? xcode kholo
  • Common commands run instantly, even before the AI model loads
  • Say it any way, in any language: the model works out which command you mean
  • Risky actions like quitting apps or shutting down can ask before running

Writing tools

Select nothing. Press a key. It's rewritten.

Click into a text field and press a shortcut. Duck Keyboard copies the text, rewrites it with the local AI and pastes the result back in place.

  • Four built in: fix grammar, write as email, professional, and rude
  • Every prompt and shortcut can be changed
  • Add your own actions, like “Translate to Hindi” or “Make it shorter”
  • Before and after are kept in History

Meetings

Notes, to-dos and who said what

Double-tap the dictation key when a call starts. Your mic and the Mac's audio are recorded, even with headphones on, so Zoom, Meet and Teams calls are covered.

  • Background noise and speaker echo are reduced
  • Speakers are labelled; name them once and the notes update
  • Summary, key points, decisions and to-dos written by the local AI
  • Play the audio from any line, or export everything as Markdown

Get started

Install in a minute

  1. Download the DMGFrom the latest release on GitHub.
  2. Drag it to Applications and open itThe first time, macOS asks: open System Settings › Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.
  3. Follow the short setupAllow the microphone and Accessibility, pick your keys, and download a speech model.
  4. Hold right ⌥ and talkThe Learn screen in the app shows everything else you can do.
